Questions, answered plainly.

Why is the core free?

Every input comes from free public sources — FRED, SEC EDGAR and others — so there's no data bill to pass on.

What does Pro add?

Convenience only: higher daily AI limits, alerts and exports. The analysis is identical on both plans.

How is billing handled?

By Stripe. Card details never reach our servers, and you can cancel anytime from the billing portal.

Is this investment advice?

No. Scora is an educational research tool. Backtests are hypothetical and capital is at risk.
