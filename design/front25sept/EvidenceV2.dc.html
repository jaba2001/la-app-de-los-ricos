How to read this page.

  • Every number comes from a committed research artifact; the test suite fails if the shipped constants drift from it.
  • The smallest effect this universe can detect is an IC of 0.044 at 80% power. Below it, a coefficient isn't weak — it's undetectable, and grades score it as zero.
  • The headline compares against SPY. The same signal can beat SPY and still lose to its own equal-weighted universe.
  • Grades penalise multiplicity: every trial on the same history raises the bar for all of them.
  • Past measurement is not a forecast. Nothing here is investment advice.
