What these numbers do not say
  • The Sharpe edge is not statistically significant. Reaching t = 2 would take roughly 17 more years.
  • The drawdown is a different kind of claim. -16% against -51% is what happened.
  • But “a third of the drawdown” leans on 2008.
  • And the Sharpe does not survive deflation for the number of strategies we tried.
  • Returns are not normally distributed, and the Sharpe ratio assumes they are. Excess kurtosis +2.516.
  • Cash earns the T-bill rate here, not zero.
